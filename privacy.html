Privacy policy

In effect from 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect on veriapv.com and in the Veria app, why, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. We process personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Polish data protection law.

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Igor Malchenko, trading as Veria, ul. Sarmacka 19/138, 02-972 Warsaw, Poland, NIP 9512574134 ("Veria", "we", "us").

For anything about your data, write to igor@coolstory.me or to the address above. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as the law does not require one for our activities. Igor Malchenko handles all data protection matters directly.

2. When we are the controller, and when we are not

Veria is used by pharmaceutical companies and the people who keep their PSMFs (our "customers"). The content customers put into Veria, such as their PSMF and the names and details of the people it describes, belongs to the customer. For that content, the customer is the controller and we process it only on their instructions, as their processor, under our data processing agreement. If your details appear in a customer's PSMF and you want to exercise your rights, please contact that customer. We will help them answer you.

This policy covers the data we decide about ourselves: visits to our website, demo requests and correspondence, user accounts and sign-in security, and our business relationship with customers.

3. What we collect and why

Visiting our website

When you open veriapv.com, our hosting provider receives the technical data any browser sends: your IP address, browser and device type, the page requested and the time. This is needed to deliver the page and keep it secure (legal basis: our legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We measure how the website is used with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not identify you: each visit is counted with a code derived from the request that resets every 24 hours, so visits cannot be linked across days or websites. We use the totals to improve the site (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f)).

Booking a demo and writing to us

When you book a demo or email us, we receive your name, email address and anything you choose to tell us, such as your company and role. Demos may be booked through Calendly or Google Calendar, which then process your booking details for us. We use this to arrange the demo, answer you and follow up on a possible contract (steps at your request before a contract, Article 6(1)(b), and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, Article 6(1)(f)).

Using the Veria app

To give a customer's people access to the app, we process each user's name, work email address, organisation and role, the one-time sign-in codes we send, and security records of sign-ins (time, IP address, browser). When a user signs a PSMF version, we record who signed, when and how they verified it. We process this to provide the service to the customer, keep accounts secure and keep a reliable record of signatures (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f), and, where you are our customer's contact person, the contract, Article 6(1)(b)).

We also send service emails: sign-in codes, questions in a revision, and notices about the service. These are part of the service and cannot be switched off while you have an account.

Customers and billing

For customers, we keep the company's details, the names and contact details of its contact people, the contract and invoices. We need this to perform the contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and to meet our accounting and tax obligations (legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c)).

Do you have to give us your data?

No law requires you to. Without an email address we cannot arrange a demo, and without the account details above we cannot give you access to the app.

5. Cookies

Our website sets no cookies. If you book a demo through an embedded Calendly page, Calendly may set its own cookies, which are needed for the booking to work; Calendly's policy explains them.

The app uses only what is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and secure. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies anywhere, so we don't ask for cookie consent.

6. Who receives your data

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers who process it for us under contract and on our instructions:

  • GitHub (Microsoft) and Vercel, which host our website and app, and Vercel's web analytics
  • Supabase, which stores the app's data in Frankfurt, Germany
  • Resend, which sends our emails
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI features in the app
  • Calendly and Google, if you book a demo through them
  • Our own email provider, our accountant and our legal advisers, where needed

The providers that process customer data in the app are listed, with their locations, on our subprocessors page. We may also disclose data to courts and public authorities when the law requires it.

7. Where your data is stored

The app's data is stored in the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany. Some of our providers are based in the United States, and some processing, such as sending emails and running AI features, takes place there. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is protected in one of two ways the GDPR recognises:

  • EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The European Commission has decided that US companies certified under this framework protect personal data adequately (Article 45 GDPR).
  • Standard contractual clauses. Contract terms approved by the European Commission that bind the recipient to EU data protection standards (Article 46 GDPR).

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply.

8. AI in the app

The app uses Anthropic's AI models to sort answers, draft text and flag problems in a PSMF. Text is sent to Anthropic only to produce that result. Anthropic does not use it to train its models. Every AI suggestion is reviewed by a person before it takes effect. We make no decisions about people based solely on automated processing.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted, or its use restricted
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what was done before

To use any of these rights, email igor@coolstory.me. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl), or to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when what we do with data changes. The date at the top shows the current version. We will tell customers about significant changes by email before they take effect.
