2 Organisational structure of the MAH

2.1 Marketing authorisation holder

Halvorsen Pharma A/S is a privately owned company in Hellerup, Denmark. It holds marketing authorisations for 14 medicinal products in the EEA and the United Kingdom.

2.2 Pharmacovigilance department

The PV department in Hellerup reports to the Chief Medical Officer. Case processing, literature screening and medical information are delegated to Nordlys Safety Services (Annex B.1).

2.3 Distributors and licence partners

Halvorsen Pharma distributes its products in the Nordic and Baltic markets through Medica Nord AS, Nordvia AB and Baltpharm UAB. Each distributor forwards adverse event reports within one business day under its safety data exchange agreement (Annex B.2).

2.4 Delegated activities

Delegated activities and the oversight of each provider are described in Annex B.3. The QPPV reviews provider performance quarterly against the indicators in Annex F.

5 Pharmacovigilance processes

5.1 Collection of safety reports

Reports reach Halvorsen through medical information, distributors, the literature and national competent authorities. Each is forwarded to Nordlys Safety Services within one business day (SOP-PV-003).

5.2 Case processing

Nordlys Safety Services processes individual case safety reports and submits them to EudraVigilance and the MHRA within 15 or 90 days (VP-SOP-012).

5.3 Literature screening

Global literature is searched weekly. Local journals in the Nordic and Baltic markets are screened monthly (VP-SOP-029).

5.4 Signal management

Signals are reviewed monthly by the PV department and discussed at the quarterly safety committee (SOP-PV-011).

5.5 Periodic safety update reports

PSURs follow the EU reference dates list. The QPPV approves each report before submission (SOP-PV-014).
