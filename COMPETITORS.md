Method: homepage plus one or two key pages per site. One entry per competitor, committed as soon as it's written. Quotes are short and verbatim; everything else is paraphrase.

## Entries

### Veeva Vault SafetyDocs
Pages: `/products/vault-safety/`, `/products/vault-safetydocs/` (Oct 2026)

- **Headline promise:** category labels, not promises. "Regulated Safety Content and Process Management." Safety: "Global Adverse Event Management and Oversight."
- **Vision:** one global system for all of safety. AI agents take routine work. A customer's quote carries the ambition ("safety intelligence unit"), not Veeva's own voice.
- **The "after":** less manual document assembly, always inspection-ready. Calm, never emotional.
- **Results claimed:** almost none of their own. The one number comes from a Moderna quote: merged PSMF PDF in "less than 30 minutes" instead of hours.
- **PSMF framing:** a document-management problem (binders, structure that matches GVP Module II, global and local variants, reuse). Assembly and storage, not keeping facts true.
- **Tone:** enterprise, flat, safe. "Validated", "inspection readiness", "operational efficiency".
- **Trust devices:** big pharma logos, named exec testimonials, "Mature" product status, release cadence ("3 releases a year"), trust page, community.
- **For Veria:** Veeva sells the shelf the PSMF sits on. It never claims the content is right. "The file is true" is open ground. Their proof is scale and logos, which we can't match. Lean on the people who built it and the controls instead. Also: their strongest line is borrowed from a customer. Bold vision reads safer in pharma when it's framed as what a QPPV would want.

### Extedo
Pages: homepage, `/software/pharmacovigilance-and-drug-safety` (Oct 2026)

- **Headline promise:** "effortless compliance", repeated everywhere ("Effortless Safety Case Management", "Your plan to effortless compliance").
- **Vision:** compliance gets simple and stays simple. Faster time to market. Generic; it could sit on any RIM vendor's site.
- **The "after":** fewer errors and missed deadlines, and peace of mind ("you know that your safety cases are being managed accurately").
- **Results claimed:** stat bar: >98% retention, >70% of eCTD authorities, >50% faster submission compilation, >1M submissions; intake "up to 70%" faster, validated "within two weeks".
- **PSMF framing:** barely there. One mention, as a consulting deliverable. No PSMF product on the site despite the reputation.
- **Tone:** problem-first questions ("How much time are you wasting…?"), then a three-step plan. A classic StoryBrand template.
- **Trust devices:** EMA logo next to pharma logos, 25+ years, used by 35+ agencies.
- **For Veria:** "effortless" is taken and has gone hollow. No one owns the PSMF as a product at this level. Their strongest trust device is "regulators use us". Ours is the nearest equivalent: built by people who did QPPV work, designed around what inspectors flag.
